## Acceptance criteria

- **BAH-45-01:** complete bounded frozen inventory across pages; no omitted/duplicate/foreign members; cap exceeded reports incomplete coverage rather than a false complete review.
- **BAH-45-02:** full immutable wording/evidence and correction/base bindings, exact source provenance; truncated previews and missing evidence are not approval-ready.
- **BAH-45-03:** sealed reviewed revisions/selection exclude later arrivals and changed content; independent-human decision, rationale/evidence and exact reviewed version remain enforced for every item.
- **BAH-45-04:** classification is distinct from committed action; duplicate/outdated recommendations never delete originals or grant an unspecified close/merge operation.
- **BAH-45-05:** same-key replay returns original scope/actor-bound receipt under current authority; changed input conflicts; lost response/fresh process neither duplicates semantic state nor fabricates success.
- **BAH-45-06:** concurrent decisions, stale bases, expiry, foreign identity, raw/unlinked evidence and revocation deny affected work/disclosure with no unauthorized effect. Preserve completed receipts and current-replay access checks.
- **BAH-45-07:** exact item outcomes reconcile to frozen membership; unconfirmed remains unconfirmed; needs_evidence remains pending; later arrivals/current queue are separate observations.
- **BAH-45-08:** append-only metadata/receipts and decision/semantic atomicity survive injected durable-boundary faults; no-change produces no capture/proposal solely for maintenance.
