## 1. Brand Foundations (from Strategy Pack)

- **Archetype:** Caregiver (50%) + Creator (30%) + Explorer (20%)
- **Personality:** Sincerity (4.5/5) + Competence (3.5/5)
- **Brand Promise:** A thoughtful balance of heart, mind, and imagination.
- **Tagline:** MEMORY THAT SHOWS ITS WORK
- **Core Visual Essence:** Warm + Connected — geometric construction with organic warmth

---

## 2. Logo Philosophy

Allura's logo system is built on **geometric clarity** balanced with **human warmth**. Every letterform is constructed from circles, rectangles, and organic overlaps — the same shapes used throughout the brand's visual language. The wordmark feels **crafted** (Creator) and **trustworthy** (Caregiver), while the lettermark communicates **connection** through abstract overlap.

The design avoids overly technical or sterile geometry; instead, it uses slightly imperfect, hand-finished proportions that feel warm and human. The signature **blue circle + orange circle + green shape** motif appears as an embedded element within the letterforms, creating a layered, transparent quality that visually represents the brand's promise of **memory that shows its work**.

## 3. The 5-Direction Framework (Pre-Validated)

The following logo system was already designed and extracted from the approved Figma brand kit. The Team Durham pipeline validates each direction against the strategic framework.

### Direction 1: Archetype-True — "The Connector"

**Concept:** The purest expression of Allura's Caregiver archetype — a mark that feels like it *wraps around* and *protects* what connects us. The overlapping circles in the wordmark create a sense of embrace and care.

**Alignment:**
- **Primary (Caregiver):** Soft, enveloping geometry. The letterforms feel protective and accessible.
- **Secondary (Creator):** The custom construction of each letter shows intentional craft.

**Visual Elements:**
- **Shape language:** Circles, rounded rectangles, overlapping transparency
- **Color palette:**
  - Primary: `#111827` (Charcoal/Dark Green) — authoritative yet warm
  - Secondary: `#1D4ED8` (Blue) — trust, clarity
  - Accent: `#FF5A2E` (Orange) — warmth, care
  - Accent: `#157A4A` (Green) — growth, memory
  - Accent: `#C89B3C` (Gold) — premium, empowerment
- **Typography:** Custom geometric letterforms for "allura" — no off-the-shelf font
- **Symbol/Mark:** Intersecting circles integrated into letterforms (signature motif)

**Scale:**
- **Large (hero):** Perfectly balanced; the overlapping shapes create depth and visual interest at scale.
- **Medium (header):** Letterforms hold up well; clear space around logo is visible.
- **Small (favicon):** Simplified to lettermark; works at 16px–32px.
- **Monochrome:** Works in single color; the shapes' transparency is replaced with solid fills or outline strokes.

**Differentiation:** No other memory/AI brand uses custom geometric letterforms with embedded multi-color shape motifs.
**Risks:** The color complexity makes it harder to reproduce on certain materials (embroidery, low-res screens). The monochrome version may lose some of the embedded detail.

---

### Direction 2: Archetype-Contrast — "The Explorer's Path"

**Concept:** Lean into the Explorer archetype (20%) by creating a mark that suggests forward motion and discovery. The letterforms angle slightly or the embedded shapes suggest forward arrows.

**Alignment:**
- **Primary (Explorer):** Open, adventurous geometry. Suggests looking forward.
- **Note:** This was explored but **rejected** by the brand team as too aggressive for Allura's positioning. The final wordmark retains a *subtle* forward lean but remains balanced.

**Visual Elements (as implemented):**
- **Shape language:** Slight forward lean to the "a" and forward-pointing angle in "r"
- **Color palette:** Same core palette with gold (`#C89B3C`) emphasized to signal aspiration.

**Risks:** Too much lean breaks the Caregiver warmth. The final wordmark uses only a *hint* of this direction.

---

### Direction 3: Category-Disruptive — "The Memory Layer"

**Concept:** Break from the SaaS/startup tendency toward generic sans-serif wordmarks. Allura's custom typeface and embedded color shapes deliberately stand apart from competitors like Mem.ai or Notion.

**Alignment:**
- **Differentiation:** Custom geometric letterforms are extremely rare in the AI/memory category.
- **Category convention:** Competitors (Mem.ai, Rewind.ai, Obsidian) use off-the-shelf geometric sans-serifs. Allura's custom mark signals a higher level of craft and care.

**Visual Elements (as implemented):**
- **Shape language:** Unique letterform construction — not from any existing typeface.
- **Color palette:** Multi-color embedded shapes (blue + teal + orange) in a single wordmark — uncommon and distinctive.

**Risks:** Custom typefaces can be harder to update. The mark requires careful color management.

---

### Direction 4: Minimalist Essence — "The Monogram"

**Concept:** Strip away everything except the essential idea of "connection + memory + clarity." The AL lettermark represents this direction.

**Alignment:**
- **Minimalism:** Reduced to two letters abstracted into overlapping geometric forms.
- **Core idea:** The "A" and "L" interlock to suggest memory as a **chain of connections**.

**Visual Elements:**
- **Shape language:** Two letterforms overlapped at a precise angle to form a unified symbol.
- **Color palette:** Single color (Charcoal or Blue) for maximum reduction.
- **Scale:** Specifically designed for 16px–64px app icons and favicons.

**Risks:** At very large sizes, the single-color monogram lacks the warmth and depth of the full wordmark. Best used as an app icon or shortcut mark, never as a primary brand mark.

---

### Direction 5: Aspirational Stretch — "The Sovereign Mark"

**Concept:** Where might Allura evolve in 3–5 years as it scales into enterprise AI and sovereign memory governance? A mark that feels slightly more institutional, more premium, yet retains human warmth.

**Alignment:**
- **Aspirational:** The lockup "Allura Memory" with the gold bar beneath suggests a more established, product-line-capable identity.
- **Stretch:** Retains core typeface but introduces a formal structure (gold bar + dark background) that would work in enterprise decks and pitch materials.

**Visual Elements (as implemented):**
- **Style:** "Allura memory" wordmark on a dark background with a gold horizontal rule beneath.
- **Usage:** Product sub-brand, enterprise landing pages, investor decks.

**Risks:** The gold bar lockup can feel heavy if overused. Strictly for product-context usage.

## 4. Logo Construction

### 4.1 Wordmark Construction

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  a  l  l  u  r  a                                       │
│  ┌─┐ ┌─────────┐ ┌───┐  ┌────┐                         │
│  │o│ │  circle │ │ l │  │  o │  ← Overlapping shapes   │
│  └─┘ └─────────┘ └───┘  └────┘    embedded into forms   │
│     (blue)  (teal)  (orange)     ← Color accents       │
│                                                         │
│  Base color: Charcoal/Dark Green (#111827)             │
│  Clear space: X = height of "ll" pair                  │
│  Minimum size: 32px width                              │
│  Custom typeface: Non-commercial, brand-only           │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Technical:**
- **Wordmark file:** `brand-assets/logos/wordmark.png` (618×400)
- **Format:** PNG (raster) + vector source needed in Figma
- **Clear space:** Minimum distance around logo = X (height of "ll" tall letters)
- **Minimum size:** 32px on screen / 10mm in print

### 4.2 Lettermark Construction

```
┌─────────────────────┐
│    ┌───┐            │
│   ╱  A  ╲           │
│  │    L   │         │
│   ╲      ╱            │
│    └───┘            │
│  400×400 px         │
│  Abstract AL        │
│  monogram           │
└─────────────────────┘
```

**Technical:**
- **Lettermark file:** `brand-assets/logos/lettermark-AL.png` (400×400)
- **Usage:** App icons, favicons, small marks
- **Minimum size:** 24px
- **Color:** Primarily charcoal/dark green, with optional single-color variations

### 4.3 Lockup Construction — "Allura Memory"

```
Dark background (#111827)
┌──────────────────────────┐
│   Allura memory          │
│   ───────────────        │
│   (gold bar accent)      │
│   #C89B3C               │
└──────────────────────────┘
```

**Technical:**
- **Usage:** Product pages, sub-brand context, investor decks
- **Color context:** Designed for dark backgrounds only
- **Restrictions:** Never on light backgrounds; never without the gold bar

## 7. Clear Space & Minimum Sizes

### Clear Space
- **Rule:** Minimum clear space around the wordmark = **X**, where X is the height of the lowercase letters "ll" in the wordmark.
- **Application:** No other text, icons, or visual elements may enter the clear space zone.

### Minimum Sizes
| Logo | Screen (px) | Print (mm) |
|------|------------|------------|
| Wordmark | 32px | 10mm |
| Lettermark | 24px | 8mm |
| Lockup | 48px | 12mm |

---

## 8. Incorrect Usage (Do Not)

| Restriction | Example of Violation |
|------------|---------------------|
| **Do not recolor** | Changing the charcoal base to purple or red |
| **Do not stretch** | Horizontally or vertically distorting the wordmark |
| **Do not rotate** | Tilting or rotating the logo off-axis |
| **Do not alter the lockup** | Removing the gold bar from "Allura Memory" or adding unauthorized elements |
| **Do not add effects** | Drop shadows, gradients, 3D bevels, glows |
| **Do not use low-res** | Using the PNG below 32px without the lettermark |
| **Do not crowd** | Placing text or imagery within the clear space |

## 9. Application Examples

### Website Header
- **Left-aligned** wordmark at 48px height
- **Background:** Light (`#F5F1E6` cream) or dark (`#111827` charcoal)
- **Spacing:** 24px margin above, 24px below

### App Icon (iOS + Android)
- **Lettermark-only** at 1024×1024px
- **Background:** Charcoal (`#111827`) or Blue (`#1D4ED8`)
- **Corner radius:** iOS 22.5% (180px), Android adaptive (varies by shape)

### Social Media Profile
- **Lettermark AL** centered in a 400×400px circular crop
- **Background:** Cream (`#F5F1E6`) or Charcoal (`#111827`)

### Investor Deck Title Slide
- **Lockup** "Allura memory" centered on dark background
- **Gold bar accent** beneath wordmark
- **Typographic hierarchy:** Lockup at 15% of slide height
