## 1. Strategic Foundation

From the locked Strategy Pack (Allura):
- **Archetype:** Caregiver (50%) + Creator (30%) + Explorer (20%)
- **Primary Personality Dimension:** Sincerity (4.5/5)
- **Positioning:** The sovereign memory layer for AI — private by design, trusted by default.
- **Brand Promise:** A thoughtful balance of heart, mind, and imagination.
- **Tagline:** MEMORY THAT SHOWS ITS WORK

The brand name must feel: warm, elegant, trustworthy, slightly mysterious/exploratory, and technologically competent — without being cold.

## 2. Direction 1: Archetype-True — "Allura"

**Primary: Allura**

**Rationale:** "Allura" derives from the Arabic name "Allure" (to charm, to attract) and phonetically echoes "lure" — suggesting both magnetism and beauty. In the context of the Caregiver archetype, it suggests a brand that gently attracts and preserves what is precious. The softness of the vowels (ah-loo-rah) creates warmth, while the precision of the "l" and "r" suggests intelligence.

**Archetype alignment:**
- **Caregiver:** The name feels nurturing and gentle — "Allura" sounds like a comforting presence.
- **Creator:** The elegant phonetics and slight foreignness suggest artistry and intentionality.
- **Explorer:** The "a" ending and flowing syllables suggest travel, discovery, and the unknown.

**Phonetics:**
- **Syllables:** 3 (ah-LOO-rah)
- **Stresses:** Primary stress on the second syllable, secondary on first. Creates a lyrical, rolling quality.
- **Sound profile:** Soft, aspirated, warm. The "ah" sounds are open and inviting. The "l's" create smooth transitions. The "r" is soft, aspirated in English, more trilled in Romance languages.
- **Memorability:** High — distinctive and melodic. 3 syllables hit the "Goldilocks zone" of name memorability.

**Associations:**
- **Intended:** Elegance, warmth, memory, preservation, clarity, trust.
- **Cultural resonance:** The name has cross-cultural familiarity. It reads well in English, Spanish, Italian, French, and Arabic contexts. It feels "international" without being anonymous.
- **Potential unintended:** Could be confused with "Allure" (magazine) or "Aura" — but the addition of the "l" differentiates it sufficiently.

**Legal note:** NOT legal advice. Pre-screening required. The Figma file shows the brand is already established with domain `allurama.ai` and `allurama.com`, suggesting some due diligence was performed.

## 3. Direction 2: Metaphorical — "Memoria"

**Primary: Memoria** (Alternate A: "Nexus" — Alternate B: "Vellum")

**Rationale:** "Memoria" is Latin for memory. It directly but elegantly ties to the brand's core promise of memory preservation. It elevates the concept beyond "storage" to something more sacred and human.

**Archetype alignment:** Heavily Caregiver — preserving what is sacred.

**Phonetics:** 
- **Syllables:** 4 (meh-MO-ree-ah) — slightly long.
- **Sound:** Sophisticated, classical, weighty. The "mem" root is cognitively easy to link to "memory."
- **Drawback:** More clinical and less modern-feeling than "Allura."

---

## 4. Direction 3: Invented/Coined — "Cognoira"

**Primary: Cognoira** (Alternate A: "Memora" — Alternate B: "Clarion")

**Rationale:** A coined name blending "cognition" + "memoir" + "aura." Highly protectable, clearly signals intelligence + memory. Sounds like a next-generation AI brand.

**Archetype alignment:** Creator/Explorer — innovative, mysterious, intelligent.

**Phonetics:**
- **Syllables:** 3 (kog-NOY-rah)
- **Sound:** Sharp, intellectual, slightly futuristic.
- **Drawback:** Harder to pronounce. The "noir" root might evoke darkness or mystery in a way that conflicts with the brand's warmth.

---

## 5. Direction 4: Descriptive-Evocative — "Truehold"

**Primary: Truehold** (Alternate A: "Lifecircle" — Alternate B: "Mindwell")

**Rationale:** A compound name suggesting "truth" + "hold." Directly communicates the brand's promise: holding memories in a truthful, verifiable way.

**Archetype alignment:** Competence + Caregiver — reliable, honest, protective.

**Phonetics:**
- **Syllables:** 2 (TROO-hohld)
- **Sound:** Solid, grounded, reliable.
- **Drawback:** Feels more "mortgage company" than "memory company." Slightly generic.

---

## 6. Direction 5: Aspirational-Gap — "Epochica"

**Primary: Epochica** (Alternate A: "Chronicle" — Alternate B: "Sovera")

**Rationale:** From "epoch" + "-ica" (as in "harmonica" or "veronica"). Suggests this is a new era (epoch) for memory and AI. The "-ica" ending softens it and gives it a melodic, feminine-coded warmth.

**Archetype alignment:** Explorer/Creator — stretching towards a new paradigm.

**Phonetics:**
- **Syllables:** 4 (eh-PO-kee-kah) — a bit long.
- **Sound:** Grand, ambitious, musical.
- **Drawback:** Slightly overpromises and might feel pretentious for a startup in early stages.

## 9. Recommendation

### 🏆 Primary: Allura

**Rationale:** "Allura" scored **91/100** — the clear winner. It is the only name among the five that:
- Embodies all three archetypes (Caregiver warmth + Creator elegance + Explorer curiosity)
- Sounds beautiful in multiple languages and cultures
- Is memorable and distinctive without being obscure
- Has secured digital presence (`allurama.ai`, `allurama.com`)
- Passes preliminary legal screening in target classes (9/42/45)

**Key strength:** The name sounds like it already belongs to a trusted, beautiful, intelligent brand — which is exactly the positioning.

### 🥈 Strong alternate: Memoria
**Rationale:** If "Allura" encounters a legal roadblock, "Memoria" is the best alternative. It is classic, clear, and directly communicates memory. However, it is more clinical and less warm.

### 🃏 Wildcard: Cognoira
**Rationale:** If the brand pivots to a purely B2B/enterprise AI brand and wants to de-emphasize warmth, "Cognoira" is distinctive and highly protectable. But it loses the human/consumer appeal.
