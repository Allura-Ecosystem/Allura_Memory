## Brand Purpose

To give people the power to hold on to what matters and share it with the people who matter most.

**Brand Belief:** We believe memories deserve more than storage. They deserve clarity, connection and care — today and for generations to come.

**Brand Essence:** Warm + Connected

Allura exists at the intersection of memory and technology — designed to help people hold on to what matters and share it with the people who matter most. We combine intelligent design with human understanding to create experiences that are simple, meaningful and lasting.

**Brand Promise:** A thoughtful balance of heart, mind and imagination. That's how we make memory matter.

Allura is a modern, human-centered technology brand that brings clarity to the moments that matter.

We help people preserve, connect and empower their memories through intelligent solutions that are simple, trustworthy and built for real life.

## Logo System

### 1. Wordmark (Primary)
- **File:** `logos/wordmark.png` (618x400)
- **Usage:** Primary logo — horizontal orientation
- **Minimum size:** 32px
- **Clear space:** X = height of the "ll"
- **Style:** Custom geometric letterforms spelling "allura" in lowercase. Dark green base with blue and teal accent shapes integrated into the letterforms.

### 2. Lettermark / Monogram (AL)
- **File:** `logos/lettermark-AL.png` (400x400)
- **Usage:** App icon, monogram — use sparingly
- **Minimum size:** 24px
- **Description:** Abstract AL monogram designed to represent connection, memory and clarity.

### 3. Lockup — "Allura Memory"
- **Usage:** Product, campaigns and sub-brands
- **Style:** Gold bar accent beneath "Allura memory" text on dark background

### Incorrect Usage (Do Not)
- Do not recolor
- Do not stretch
- Do not rotate
- Do not alter the lockup

## Design Language

- **Geometric construction:** All brand elements built from circles, rectangles, and organic overlaps
- **Overlapping forms:** Transparency and layering create depth
- **Warm + Bold:** Institutional dark green balanced with warm, approachable accent colors
- **Human-centered iconography:** Value icons use simple line art in colored circles
- **Rounded corners:** 12px border radius on containers
- **Signature motif:** Overlapping blue circle + orange circle + green shape (appears in logos, backgrounds, and decorative elements)
