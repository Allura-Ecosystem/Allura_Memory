Observed pattern: a concrete example, a useful distinction, consequences, then
judgment. Weigh genuine arguments on both sides; acknowledge uncertainty and
mistakes. Warmth and pleasure in craft matter as much as architectural restraint.
The resulting examples are original, not quotations or claims of lived experience.

## Why voice can disappear

Local inspection found an older project Brooks definition and matching adapters
overriding newer plugin guidance. Compulsory templates also encouraged log-like
delivery. Approved Brain record `5f9caf69-c1a0-42bb-a4f3-b02840e67992` describes
historical definition drift on 2026-08-28; it corroborates the failure pattern,
not current configuration or authority. Its old connection advice is not policy.

Primary research supports checking behavior rather than only persona keywords:

- [Persistent Personas?](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.12775): persona fidelity can
  erode in extended goal-oriented interactions.
- [Measuring and Controlling Persona Drift](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.10962v1):
  investigates drift and model-level attention interventions. Our prompt repair
  does not implement those model-level methods.
- [PersonaGym](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.368/): evaluates persona
  behavior across varied situations; a bigger model alone is not a fidelity test.
- [Official Codex prompting guide](https://developers.openai.com/cookbook/examples/gpt-5/codex_prompting_guide):
  personality guidance and instruction assembly are relevant to delivery. Local
  source precedence was checked separately; no provider/model changes were needed.

## Acceptance

Keep a bounded, early voice capsule; preserve reasoning in ordinary answers;
use metaphors selectively and accurately. Check adapter parity, unchanged runtime
fields, and a fresh behavioral scenario without prompting specific catchphrases.
Static checks do not prove personality quality, and a single scenario does not
prove long-session retention. No hydration latency or token savings are claimed
without a timed runtime measurement.
