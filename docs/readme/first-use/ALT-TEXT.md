# Text alternatives

These descriptions give the full meaning independently of the image or its colors. Desktop flow diagrams read left to right; phone flow diagrams read top to bottom. The responsibility graphic contains four roles, not a causal sequence.

## Remember a decision

**A decision your next session can find.** A connected agent submits the example decision “We chose PostgreSQL,” together with its reason. A later connected session searches for the project decision. The reader inspects the returned context, source and available review state. An integration must explicitly call save and search. This illustrates the intended useful task; no live round-trip success or correct next answer is guaranteed by the drawing.

## Who does what

**Who does what?** The agent or integration calls save and search and chooses what context to submit. Allura stores submitted context and retrieves within authorized scope. The operator sets up hosting and access and maintains the service. An independent authorized human decides on eligible promotion proposals. Installing Allura does not automatically capture every conversation. Routine memory calls and human promotion approval are different responsibilities.

## Saved versus approved

**Saved does not mean approved.** A saved record is retained evidence, not human approval. Eligible content may be queued as a proposal; not every save creates one. Independent human approval and canonical materialization produce approved knowledge under the intended contract. A rejected proposal retains evidence without an approved label. A confidence score cannot substitute for a human decision. Inspect the source and actual review state; missing state remains unknown. The drawing does not certify all implementation paths or close outstanding reviewed-version, lineage or delivery gaps.
