# Allura Evidence Infographics — Alt Text

1. **Every action leaves proof:** A connected path links a request, a review, and a durable record. A note says the source, decision, and result stay linked.
2. **Access has boundaries:** Two labeled workspaces sit on either side of a policy-boundary lock, showing that private work remains in its own workspace.
3. **People review important changes:** A person-centered review module connects a proposed change, a policy check, and a recorded decision.
4. **Memory keeps its history:** A curved path connects saved context, a review, and approved memory, with a note that older records stay connected.
5. **Test before release:** A loop links the same scenario, a run, a result review, and saved proof, showing that the process repeats when the system changes.
6. **An answer can show its work:** A central answer module points to factual source, review decision, and approved context, showing that a good answer can link back to its support.

## Color legend

- Blue: memory and intelligence
- Orange: review and decision
- Green: approved knowledge and connection
- Ink: governance and structure

These graphics describe evidence-first engineering patterns. They make no formal compliance, certification, or external assurance claim.
